Privacy Policy

This page describes how this site handles visitor information. It lists the services actually in use — not a generic template.

Analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc., United States).

  • Collected: time of visit, page viewed, referrer, approximate country or region, browser type
  • Purpose: to see which posts are read, so the site can be improved
  • No cookies: this analytics does not store an identifying cookie in your browser and does not collect information that identifies you personally
  • See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details

One caveat worth stating, since this site is about being honest with numbers: measurement of this kind counts automated traffic such as search engine crawlers alongside human readers, so the visitor figures are an upper bound rather than a count of people.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to the email list, your email address is stored by the newsletter provider in order to send you notifications of new posts.

  • Collected: the email address you enter
  • Purpose: notifying you when new posts are published
  • Third parties: your address is not shared or sold
  • International transfer: the address is stored on the provider's servers, which are located outside your country
  • Unsubscribing: every email contains an unsubscribe link. Once you unsubscribe, your address is removed from the sending list

Advertising

This site currently carries no advertising. If ads are added, this page will be updated with the provider's name and cookie behaviour first.

Affiliate links

This site currently contains no affiliate links. If a post ever contains one, a disclosure will appear at the top of that post.

Retention, access and deletion

Email addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. If you want to see, correct or delete information held about you, get in touch and it will be handled.

Changes

Any change to what is collected will be reflected here before it takes effect.
