About

What this site is

I'm not an engineer. I write here about using AI coding tools — mostly Claude Code — to build small automated income streams, and I publish what actually happened rather than what was supposed to happen.

This site is itself one of the experiments. Part of the writing, building and publishing pipeline is automated, and the record of that pipeline breaking and getting fixed is most of the content.

How the posts are made

Drafts are produced with AI assistance and then edited before publishing. Nothing goes out unread. Where a post describes a result — a cost, a number of visitors, a thing that failed — that result actually happened to me. I don't invent case studies.

When I haven't tried something, I say so instead of writing about it as though I had.

What this site does not do

There is no "make $X in Y days" here. At the time of writing, most of what I've built earns close to nothing, and I'd rather say that plainly than pretend otherwise. If a post ever contains an affiliate link, it will say so at the top.
